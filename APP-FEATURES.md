# The Westend City Church App

One app for the whole church family: photos and videos, announcements and events, attendance, follow-up and pastoral care. It works on any phone or computer and can be installed on the home screen like a normal app.

---

## The problems it solves

| Before | With the app |
|---|---|
| Photos and videos from services were scattered across phones, WhatsApp groups and drives, and hard to find later. | One private gallery, organised by folder and event, searchable, with safe sharing links. |
| Attendance was written on paper or in spreadsheets, counted late, and often lost. Constituency totals took hours to compile. | Attendance is taken on a phone in minutes. Totals for each basonta, constituency and the whole church add up automatically. |
| Ushers had to count every person by hand, and the numbers couldn't be checked against who was actually there. | Members check themselves in by scanning a QR code. Ushers still record the head-count, and basonta sheets fill themselves in. |
| Absent members slipped through the cracks; nobody knew who had been called or visited. | Follow-ups are assigned to a named pastor or shepherd, with notes, due dates and reminders, until they're done. |
| First-time visitors left without anyone getting their details. | Visitors fill in a welcome card from a QR code. It goes straight to the leaders for a welcome call. |
| Announcements got lost in busy WhatsApp groups. | Announcements go to the right people (whole church, leaders, or particular groups), arrive as notifications, and show who has seen them. |
| Rotas lived on paper; people forgot their duty days. | Bacenta rotas in the app, with a reminder to each person the evening before. |
| Adding members one at a time was slow. | Import a whole Excel or CSV list at once, with duplicates and mistakes caught before anything is saved. |
| Inviting people to the app was one email at a time. | One QR code invites a whole group. People scan it and create their own account in a minute. |
| Birthdays and milestones were forgotten. | Birthday notifications to the person and their group leaders, a birthdays list on every group, and milestones (salvation, baptism…) on each member's record. |

## Features by person

### Every member
- **Join with a QR code.** Enter your name, phone number and a password, and choose your basonta and bacentas (both optional). Your constituency is set for you from your basonta. You're signed in straight away.
- **Sign in with your phone number or email.**
- **My church.** Your groups and their leaders (tap to call), your serving duties on the rota, birthdays coming up in your groups, and your own attendance history. Keep your phone, email, area and birthday up to date yourself.
- **Announcements.** News from the church and your groups, with an unread badge and notifications.
- **Calendar.** Church and group events, with Going / Maybe / Can't go and how many guests you're bringing. Events can have a limit on places.
- **Sunday check-in.** Scan the service QR code and tap "I'm here". Check in your children or family members with you, and add anyone else who came with you (a child, relative or friend).
- **Notifications** (optional, per device): attendance reminders, birthdays, announcements and rota duties. Choose which ones you want.
- **Photos and videos** from services and events, in the folders you have access to.
- **Privacy.** Members see their own record, their groups and their leaders, never other members' details.

### Pastors and shepherds
- **Groups:** constituencies, basontas inside them, and bacentas (ministries such as Media, Choir, Ushers) with teams and team leads. Badges show the groups you lead.
- **Members:** add, edit, move between groups, and record their details (contact, area, birthday, date joined, notes). Search and filter by constituency, basonta, bacenta, team or role. Export to CSV.
- **Import members** from Excel or CSV. The app checks every row first and tells you which will be added, which already exist (matched by phone number) and which have mistakes, such as an unknown group or an impossible date.
- **Attendance sheets.** Tick who's present, how many visitors and first-timers came, and who each member brought. People who checked themselves in are already ticked and marked "Checked in". Unticking them asks you to confirm.
- **Works with no signal.** If you save attendance offline, it's kept on the phone and sent automatically when you're back online.
- **New people.** Guests members brought, and visitor cards, with "wants to join" highlighted. Turn a guest into a member in one step.
- **Follow-ups.** Start one from a member's page, a new person, or a visitor card. Assign it to someone (they get a notification), set a due date, record how each call or visit went (To do, Contacted, Visited, Not reachable, Done), and see what's overdue.
- **Bacenta rotas.** Put members on duty for a date, service and team. Everyone on the rota is reminded the evening before.
- **Milestones** on a member's record: salvation, foundation school, baptisms, wedding, and others.
- **Check-in household.** Set who a child or a member without a phone comes with, so that person can check them in.
- **Invite a member to the app** from their member page.
- **Statistics:** attendance trends, average attendance, people brought and who became members, for each group and period.
- **Upcoming birthdays** on every group, with a one-tap WhatsApp birthday message.
- **WhatsApp and call buttons** on member pages.
- **Announcements and events** for the groups you lead.
- **Attendance reminders** (Saturday 8pm, Sunday 1pm) if your group hasn't recorded yet.

### Ushers (leaders of the service-recording bacenta)
- **Church service head-counts:** total, visitors and new converts, for Sunday services (1st and 2nd service), Swollen Sunday, and other services.
- **Self check-in for a service.** Open check-in to get a QR code for that service only (valid that day; it stops working when closed). Show it on the screen or print it. A live count shows how many people have checked in and how.

### Super Admins (church office)
- **Everything above, church-wide.**
- **Users and roles:** Super Admin, Pastor, Shepherd, Member. Control who can see and upload to each photo folder, and grant extra permissions to a role.
- **QR invites:** one QR code invites many people as members, shepherds or pastors. Limit how many people can use it and for how long, and stop it at any time. Pastor and shepherd sign-ups wait for your approval, and you get a notification when someone signs up.
- **Reset a password.** If the person has an email, they're sent a link to choose a new password. If not, you get a one-time temporary password to send them on WhatsApp, and they choose their own when they sign in.
- **Dashboard by constituency:** each constituency's total is the sum of its basontas. See which basontas haven't recorded yet, and remind their leaders with one tap.
- **Announcements** to the whole church or all leaders, pinned or scheduled for later, with "seen by X of Y".
- **Weekend numbers** pushed on Saturday and Sunday evenings.
- **Monthly report** emailed on the 1st of each month: services, attendance, new members, new people and follow-ups.
- **Pastors and shepherds list:** who leads what, who is new, and who hasn't signed in.
- **Audit log** of important actions, and **storage use** for photos and videos.

### First-time visitors (no account needed)
- **Welcome card:** scan the QR code at church and fill in your name, phone, area, how you heard about the church and a prayer request, and tick if you'd like to become a member.

## Photos and videos
- Folders such as Sunday 1st Service, Impartation Service and Bacenta Service, each with events inside.
- Upload many photos and videos at once from a phone, including large videos. Uploads continue after a dropped connection.
- Automatic thumbnails and previews, video processing, and a full-screen viewer.
- Captions, tags, search, and highlights.
- Download single items, or a whole selection as a ZIP file.
- **Share links** for people without an account, with an expiry date.
- Deleted items go to the trash first, and can be restored.

---

## Safety and privacy
- Private by default: only invited or approved people can sign in.
- Strong passwords (at least 8 characters, and checked against known leaked passwords).
- **Two-step sign-in** with an authenticator app: required for Super Admins, optional for everyone else.
- Signing out on one device, or everywhere; a forgotten password can be reset by email or by a Super Admin.
- Members only see their own details and their leaders'.
- A record of who did what (audit log), and nightly database backups, with a second copy kept off the server.

---

## Getting started
1. The church office makes a **QR invite** (Users → QR invites) and shares it on WhatsApp or prints it.
2. People scan it and create their account.
3. On Sundays, ushers open **Self check-in** on today's service and show the QR code.
4. Turn on **notifications** under Account → Notifications. On iPhone, first add the app to the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).
